Early Life of Dmitri Mendeleev

Dmitri Ivanovich Mendeleev was born on February 8, 1834, in Tobolsk, Siberia, Russia. He was the youngest of 14 children in a large family. His father, Ivan Pavlovich Mendeleev, was a teacher and director of the Tobolsk gymnasium, and his mother, Maria Dmitrievna Kornilieva, came from a family of merchants and industrialists.

When Dmitri was young, his father became blind and lost his job, forcing the family to move. His mother took over the management of a glass factory owned by her family. Despite these hardships, Mendeleev showed early signs of intelligence and curiosity.

At the age of 14, Mendeleev moved to Moscow to attend the gymnasium. He faced financial difficulties and health issues, including tuberculosis, but his determination led him to excel in his studies. He later moved to St. Petersburg to study at the Main Pedagogical Institute.

Mendeleev's early education and experiences shaped his passion for science and laid the foundation for his groundbreaking work in chemistry.
