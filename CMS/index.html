Dmitri Ivanovich Mendeleev

Dmitri Mendeleev (1834–1907) was a Russian chemist and inventor. He is best known for formulating the Periodic Law and creating the first version of the periodic table of elements.

The Periodic Table

In 1869, Mendeleev published his periodic table, arranging elements by atomic weight and predicting the existence of undiscovered elements. His work revolutionized chemistry and earned him worldwide recognition.

Timeline of Key Events

1834

Born in Tobolsk, Siberia, Russia.

1850

Moves to Moscow to attend gymnasium.

1855

Enters the Main Pedagogical Institute in St. Petersburg.

1869

Publishes the first periodic table of elements.

1871

Predicts properties of unknown elements like gallium and scandium.

1907

Dies in St. Petersburg at age 72.
